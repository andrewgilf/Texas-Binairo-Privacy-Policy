# Texas Binairo Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 30, 2026**

Texas Binairo ("the app") is an offline logic-puzzle game for iOS. This policy explains exactly what happens with your data — in short: **we don't collect any.**

## The short version

- The app has no accounts, no servers, and no sign-in.
- Everything you do in the app — puzzles, settings, statistics — stays on your device.
- We never see, receive, or share your personal data, because it never leaves your device.
- Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple.

## What the app stores (and where)

The app saves your preferences and game progress directly on your device using standard iOS app storage. This includes:

- **Settings** — sound, haptics, appearance, and gameplay options.
- **Game progress** — boards in progress, completed puzzles, and generated puzzles.
- **Statistics** — solve counts, solve times, streaks, and Trail Ride progress.
- **Subscription status** — whether Full Ride is active, so locked features open immediately.

This information exists only on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app — or using "Reset all progress" in Settings — permanently erases it.

## Data we do not collect

The app does not collect, use, transmit, store, or sell:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords
- Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics
- Location data
- Contacts, photos, or any other content on your device
- Anything else — there is no tracking of any kind

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions ("Full Ride") are purchased, processed, and managed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account, email address, or payment details — Apple does not share them with us.

To check whether you have an active subscription, the app asks Apple directly on your device using Apple's StoreKit framework. Only the yes/no result is used, and it is stored on your device only. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Network use

The app is fully playable offline. It connects to the internet only when you purchase, restore, or manage a subscription through Apple, or when iOS checks your subscription status with Apple. No gameplay data is ever sent anywhere.

If you share a completion card, iOS hands the image to the destination you choose (such as Messages or Mail). We are never involved in, and never see, what you share.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no data from anyone, no data — including children's data — is collected, used, or shared.

## Your choices and rights

You control all of the app's data, because all of it is on your device:

- **Reset everything:** Settings → "Reset all progress."
- **Delete everything:** uninstall the app.
- **Questions or requests:** email [pidginization@gmail.com](mailto:pidginization@gmail.com). Since there are no servers and no accounts, there is no data of yours on our side to access, correct, or delete — but we're glad to answer questions.
